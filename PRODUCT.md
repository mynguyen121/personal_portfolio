## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers deciding whether to interview My Nguyen for data analyst or data science roles.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for My Nguyen, University of Rochester, expected May 2028. A hiring reader should be able to scan who she is, open the projects on the page, and contact her from this one page. Success is that reader having enough to decide on an interview, and a way to reach her.

## Positioning

The page is the scan, the proof, and the contact, together. General Mills opens a proposal deck. Meliora is told on the page and has no external link.

## Operating Context

The reader lands on a full-viewport start page and scrolls to reach the rest: About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Contact. Experience is three roles the reader switches between. Contact is email, LinkedIn, and GitHub.

## Brand Commitments

The name is My Nguyen. The mark is the letters MN. No other name, logo file, or binding visual reference was set.

## Evidence on Hand

Published copy, roles, and links are in `index.html`. The resume PDF supplied the facts above. There is no portrait in the repo. Do not fabricate testimonials, headshots, extra projects, metrics, or employers.

## Product Principles

1. Hiring readers come first. A change earns its place when it helps them decide whether to interview My.
2. Keep the path intact: scan who she is, open the work, contact her from this page.
3. Published facts stay exact. Roles, dates, metrics, awards, GPA, school, and the General Mills link are not rewritten into stronger claims.
4. Contact is email, LinkedIn, and GitHub. Do not add channels.
5. The first viewport is the start page. Scrolling reveals the rest of the portfolio.
