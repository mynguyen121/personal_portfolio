# index.html

Mode: Experience

## Scope

Recruiters scan My Nguyen, open proof, and contact her from one page. The start screen is the first viewport. Scrolling is how the rest of the page appears. Resume facts stay exact. The phone number is not on the page. The University of Rochester internship links to Figshare. The General Mills deck keeps its existing link. Meliora has no external link. The about paragraph stays. No portrait, no invented metrics, no WebGL copy of Poly.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an evening desk: the first viewport is the room, and the work is a paper sheet that slides up and covers it. It refuses both the click-through START gate and a dark analytics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Navy room #071426, lamp light #f4f7fb, paper #f3f6fb, ink #102033, one data blue #1d4ed8 reserved for Contact. Instrument Serif for the name and titles, Schibsted Grotesk for UI and body. Actions and the role switcher are pills. Sheets lift with a vertical shadow. No walnut, no orange, no sky grid.

STORY: A hiring reader sees who My is, then either opens the work or her experience, or scrolls. The sheet covers the desk and reveals about, skills, three roles, three projects, and email, LinkedIn, and GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 100svh navy desk with one cool lamp pool. Fixed bar: MN at left, text links, blue Contact pill at right. Centered name at clamp(3.4rem, 7.2vw, 6rem) with a drawn underline, the existing lead beneath, then “University of Rochester · Expected May 2028”, then two centered pills: cream “See the work” and ink “See my experience”. Bottom center, “Scroll to explore” with a drawn chevron. Signature interaction: a 200svh pin holds the desk while a cream sheet with a rounded top and a lifted shadow slides over it for one viewport, then the page reads normally.

FORM: 1. Sticky desk with a paper sheet that covers it on scroll, chosen. 2. Full-viewport scroll-snap chapters. 3. A restyled click gate. The user pinned poly.app’s start-then-scroll, so no concept-seed roll. seed: none (unavailable)
